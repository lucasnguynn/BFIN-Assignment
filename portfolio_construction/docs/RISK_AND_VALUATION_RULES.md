# Loss-review policy and valuation scope

**Core decision.** Under the foundation's preferred policy, a 12% rolling 12-month *withdrawal-adjusted return loss* requires an early warning. A loss of 15% or more requires a documented investment-committee review within ten business days. These are review triggers, not loss limits or automatic sale instructions. A separate cash-flow coverage test monitors the irrevocable nominal liabilities of $2.4 million in December 2024 and $3.6 million in December 2027.

**Return convention.** For a fund with market value immediately before an external withdrawal of W at event t, calculate the subperiod return up to the withdrawal, remove W from the capital base at that instant, then calculate the next subperiod return on the reduced fund value; chain-link net returns. A mandatory foundation payment is an external withdrawal, not an investment loss. The same principle applies to any other externally authorized fund flows, if any.

**Growth assets.** Use observed adjusted-close proxies for the 2023+ equity holdings and VTI benchmark only after the policy freeze, with the first-trade-price anchor 2023-01-03. Yahoo adjusted-close is a vendor-revised total-return proxy, not realized trade evidence. During subsequent evaluation, vendor dividend and corporate-action adjustments must be audited against the no-voluntary-tender/exchange rule set at the case date; specific later events must not be incorporated retrospectively into the original decision.

**Case bonds.** Alpha/Beta/Gamma/Delta/Epsilon are fictional, with NO observed post-case-date resale quotes. For explanatory stress scenarios, use the remaining contractual cash flows and a declared constant implied 2022 YTM or a stated rate-shift path, compound discount factors consistent with annual coupon frequencies. An outstanding bond has a modeled value, and a paid coupon is transferred into foundation cash, not counted twice. At maturity, principal is used for the mandated payments. Call results modeled fund TWR, never observed fund performance. A *truly observed* 12-month total-fund TWR cannot be produced from the assignment's fictional bond quotes alone.

**Operational limitation.** Unless the group supplies a dated external term structure, a clear curve-interpolation rule and complete monthly fund marks, the 15% modeled total-fund TWR must be reported as a governance rule and scenario estimate, not a backtested historical statistic. Report realized **growth-only** TWR and the funding-coverage ledger separately.

**Stress evidence.** Uniform equity -30% with joint +100 bp bond-yield shock costs about 17.27% of original initial capital at inception, assuming no coupons/withdrawals and a one-time 10 bp initial equity entry cost. This scenario is not the trailing TWR statistic, VaR, an event probability or a guaranteed maximum loss. The chosen $0 initial operating buffer makes residual equity exposure larger; additional liquidity can lower stress loss at the cost of a higher required 2030 growth CAGR.
