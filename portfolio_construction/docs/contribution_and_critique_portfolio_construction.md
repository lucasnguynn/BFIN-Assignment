## Critique of the Final Recommendation

The recommended equity portfolio is more concentrated than a broad-market index. Sample Mean-Variance produced the highest estimated utility under the selected expected-return assumptions, risk preferences and concentration constraints. However, its advantage over Single-Index Mean-Variance is small, and sensitivity analysis and block resampling indicate that optimized weights depend on uncertain inputs.

Under revised principal matching, an illustrative 30% equity decline combined with a 100-basis-point increase in bond yields produces an immediate whole-fund loss of approximately 17.01%, while the contractual payments remain funded under the case assumptions. This exceeds the trustees’ stated area of discomfort near 15%. However, an instantaneous stress loss differs from the group’s proposed rolling 12-month withdrawal-adjusted review measure. The trustees have not approved those proposed monitoring thresholds.

The recommendation protects scheduled payments without guaranteeing the 2030 purchasing-power goal or preventing substantial investment losses. Minimum Variance or a larger cash reserve should be reconsidered if forecast confidence falls or the trustees have less capacity to tolerate losses than assumed.
