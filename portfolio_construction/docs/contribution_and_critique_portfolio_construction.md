**Member 2 critique of the final recommendation (draft, revise in own words):** The proposed equity portfolio is more concentrated than a broad index. We chose Sample Mean-Variance because it produced the highest estimated utility under a defensible but subjective historical-mean shrinkage rule and concentration limits. The margin over Single-Index MV is small; simulated parameter changes and block resampling show that optimized weights are unstable. A 30% equity-market decline can cause the whole fund's modeled initial value to fall around or beyond the group's proposed 15% rolling review level, although an instantaneous stress loss is not the same measure and trustees have not approved that rule. The trustees should therefore understand the chance of severe growth-portfolio losses, the lack of a guaranteed 2030 wealth level, and the difference between a fictional bond pricing scenario and observed growth performance. Minimum Variance or more liquidity should be reconsidered if the foundation has lower forecast confidence or less capacity for temporary loss than assumed.
