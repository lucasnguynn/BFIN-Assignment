# Data, limitations, and responsible assistance

The 2015-2022 Yahoo prices were originally obtained by the group's yfinance-based Python retrieval (the returned daily DataFrame pickles, settings, versions, UTC retrieval times, and hashes are archived). They were downloaded retrospectively in October 2026. Yahoo's `Adj Close` history may be revised for splits and dividends, which cannot restore the exact 2022 point-in-time prices. The universe was selected retrospectively; survivorship and hindsight remain limitations even though the eligible instruments existed at the case date.

`src/rebuild.py` now checks all 13 archived raw source hashes, deserializes verified team-created pandas objects under a restricted allow-list, reconstructs monthly adjusted-close returns from the archived daily records, independently reconciles those returns to the original processed panel to numerical tolerance, and computes the portfolio models. Python pickle is unsafe for untrusted data: do **not** run this script on third-party/unverified pickles. The archived data were generated by the team's own documented yfinance retrieval process. A current downloaded price series does not constitute a 2022 vintage record.

The SEC/source evidence register identifies pre-cutoff filings and ETF documentation, but their full original bytes have not all been copied into this offline archive. SEC link and filing-date checks are not equivalent to an archived filing. This is an outstanding evidence improvement for the final submission; no original source bytes or SHA-256 claims are invented.

The growth expected-return forecast equals 25% asset-specific historical mean plus 75% VTI mean; this is a *chosen prior*, not a statistically estimated superiority. The sample covariance and VTI single-index models are compared on identical 12-asset universes and concentration constraints. The use of foundation risk aversion A=3 reduces to effective growth risk aversion A × (initial equity purchase / $12m) **only under a simplified fixed funding-sleeve approximation**. Bond-equity covariance and dynamic withdrawals are not jointly optimized. A=6 is a sensitivity scenario, not a retroactive rebalance instruction.

Real factor regressions from the Kenneth French library are owned by Member 3 and **are not included as executed results here**. The Member 2 handoff consists of 2015-2022 portfolio static-exposure proxy returns and individual-security returns for MSFT and PG, plus VTI as an investable benchmark. Never conflate VTI with the French Mkt-RF factor. Initial 2023-2025 growth results and a response to the 23 October 2026 revised trustee mandate cannot be finalized before the actual course events.
